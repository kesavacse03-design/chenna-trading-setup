In practice, here is the logic for each day after signal:Day 1 after signal: OBSERVE ONLY. Do NOT enter. This is the worst entry day. Record the Day 1 candle for reference (its low becomes a key level).Day 2: First eligible entry day. Enter ONLY if Day 1 was a red/pullback candle AND Day 2 opens flat or slightly green AND the pullback held above signal day low. Entry = Day 2 Open if conditions met, otherwise wait.Day 3: OPTIMAL entry day per data. By now the initial excitement has faded and weak hands are out. If stock is still holding above signal low and shows any bullish sign (green candle, hammer, higher low vs Day 1 low), enter at Day 3 Open.Day 4-5: Last chance. If stock is consolidating near breakout level with tightening range (NR4 or NR7 forming), enter. If it has already broken below signal day low by now, the breakout has failed - do not enter.Day 6+: Signal EXPIRED. Remove from watchlist. The stock either moved without us (missed trade, that is OK) or failed (avoided a loss, that is good).Entry Price RulesBuy at: OPEN of the day AFTER the confirmation candleGap filter: If gap up &gt; 2% from prev close, SKIP (chasing). If gap down &gt; 2%, SKIP (breakdown).Maximum entry price: Signal day close + 3%. If stock has already moved &gt;3% above signal close by entry day, the risk/reward is too poor.2.3 Stop Loss RulesStructure-based stops, NOT fixed percentage. This is critical for real trading.Initial Stop Loss (at entry)Primary stop: Below the LOWEST LOW between signal day and entry day. This is the structural support the pullback created.Maximum stop distance: If this level is &gt;4% below entry, tighten to 4%. Risk management overrides structure.Minimum stop distance: If this level is &lt;1.5% below entry, widen to 1.5%. Too tight = noise will stop you out.Example: Signal day low = 1045, Day 2 low = 1038, Day 3 entry = 1055Stop = 1038 (lowest low in range) = 1.6% below entry. Valid.2.4 Trailing Stop Loss (After Entry)This is the key to letting winners run while protecting gains. Three phases:Phase 1: Capital Protection (Day 1-3 of holding)Stop remains at: Initial stop level. Do NOT move it. Give the trade room to work.Exception: If stock drops 3%+ intraday from entry on Day 1, consider cutting early. Something may be fundamentally wrong.Phase 2: Breakeven Trail (Day 4-5 of holding)If stock is at +1.5% or more: Move stop to BREAKEVEN (entry price). Now you have a risk-free trade.If stock is flat or negative: Keep original stop. Evaluate if the setup is still valid. If the stock has made a new low below signal day low, EXIT at market.Phase 3: Profit Trail (Day 6-10 of holding)Trail method: Move stop to the LOW of the previous day each morning.Day 6: Stop = Day 5 low. Day 7: Stop = Day 6 low. And so on.Alternative ATR trail: Stop = Close minus 1.5x ATR(14). This adapts to the stock&apos;s volatility.Day 10 timeout: If stop has not been hit and target not reached, exit at Day 10 close. The average max gain day is 6.3, so by Day 10 the move has likely peaked.2.5 Target / Exit RulesPrimary target: 2x ATR(14) from entry price. For a stock with ATR = 25, target = entry + 50.Secondary target: Next daily resistance level (swing high from before signal).Partial exit: Book 50% at 1.5x risk achieved. Trail remaining 50% with Phase 3 stops.Time exit: Day 10 close if neither target nor stop hit.2.6 Position SizingCapital per trade: 15,000 INR (30% of 50,000 total capital)Max concurrent positions: 3 (total exposure = 45,000 / 90%)Risk per trade: Maximum 2% of total capital = 1,000 INRIf stop is 3% below entry: Position size = 1000 / (entry * 0.03) sharesIf stop is 2% below entry: Position size = 1000 / (entry * 0.02) sharesNEVER risk more than 1,000 INR per trade.
